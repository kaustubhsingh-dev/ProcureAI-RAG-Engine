Internal Combustion Engines Sectional Committee had been approved by the Transport Engineering Division Council

In the product specification of constant speed compression ignition ( d i e s e l ) engines ( I S 10001, 10002 and 
11170), provision is there to carry out tests whenever there is any 'Functional change' in design of the engine or  its critical components However, the term 'Functional change' has not been elaborated It was, therefore, felt that  guidelines should be available to list the possible changes which may normally be carried out by the manufacturers  in critical components and provide recommendations regarding nature of tests required to be performed on the  engines when such changes in critical components are done Hence, this standard in the series of 'Methods of  test' standards has been brought out as IS 10000 (Part 13)

3 DEFINITIONS 
3.1 The definitions given in IS 10000 (Part 1) and  the following shall apply 
3.1.1 
Functional Change 
3.1.1.1 t Any change in the design of a component  which may either effect the other mating components,  or the performance or operation of the engine, shall  be considered a functional change 
Examples  a) 
Change in the bore dimension of the bearing,  and  b) 
Change in the bore of the liner 
3.1.1.2 
Style changes shall not be considered a  functional change

NOTES 
1. If Any functional change of critical ports listed in 5.1 to 5.23 is tested as per nature of prescribed test in this standard  the same functional change may be permitted to any other manufacturer subject to their basic engine is type tested as  per 3.1 of IS 11170 or IS 10001 provided the manufacturer submits the proof of the test for particular functional  change of critical pan 
2 If any functional change of standard original equipment like fuel injection equipment, piston, piston ring set, bearings,  valves, etc, and that change is for improvement in engine performance and if this functional change is subjected 10  respective nature of tests prescribed in this standard carried out by a manufacturer, same functional change may be  adopted by any other manufacturer who desires to adopt it without going in for any test 
3 If any change in fits, tolerances is done without affecting the nominal dimensions, keeping clearance fit, interference  fit, shrink fit, transition fit, force or press fit, etc, as per previous drawings, same change may be permitted without any  test 
4 In case of multi-cylinder diesel engines of modular design construction where all the models are already type approved  and functional change related to common parts/modular design construction are type approved on any one of the  models, same functional change on these components may also be considered as type approved for other engine models  of the same series having same or lower BMEP rating 
5 If any functional change of critical parts is tested as per naiure of prescribed test in this standard on higher rating of  the engine, same change may also be considered as approved for lower rating of that engine
