1 SCOPE 
This standard ( Part I ) applies to electrical insulating  varnishes containing solvent which, after solvent  removal and curing, become non-thermoplastic. 
This part of the specification contains the definitions  related to and the general requirements for such  varnishes. 
2 REFERENCES 
The referred Indian Standards are .given in 
AnnexA. 
3 DEFINITIONS 
For the purpose of this standard, the following  definitions shall apply. 
3.1 Bot Curing Varnish 
A varnish the cure of which requires the application  of heat. 
3.2 Cold Curing Varnish 
A varnish which dries andlor cures without the  application of heat. 
3.3 Finishing Varnish 
A varnish which is-applied to the surface of equipment,  or part of it, to enhance the resistance to external  influences or to improve the appearance of the  equipment. 
3.4 Impregnating Varnish 
A varnish which is capable of penetrating the interstices  within the insulating system. 
4 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS 
4.1 All material -in a consignment shall satisfy the  requirements of this Part I and those of the appropriate  specification in Part 3. 
4.2 The tests shall be carried out in accordance with 
IS 10026 (Part 2 ). 
4.3 Colour

I  supplier. 
4·.3.2 For cold curing varnish, there shall be no  appreciable change in colour after a varnish panel,  prepared in accordance with the recommendations of  the supplier, has been heated for 48 hat 90 ± 2°C in an  oven with natural ventilation. 
41.4 Conditions of Supply 
The material shall be filled into clean dry containers  which ensure adequate protection during transport,  handling and storage. The container shall be marked  with the supplier's name or trade-mark, the material  designation and grade, flash point, mass or volume,  batch number, date of manufacture and, where  appropriate, date by which the varnish must be used. 
In addition, the packages of any varnish with a flash  point below the requirement as specified in the relevaI\.t  section of Part 3 and methods of transport involved  shall be marked clearly and indelibly with the sign  denoting flammable liquid. Similar provisions must be  made when toxic substances are present.
